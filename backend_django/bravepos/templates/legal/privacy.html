Privacy Policy
Brave POS · Brave Brand Company Limited

Brave POS is a point-of-sale application used by restaurant and retail staff to take orders, accept payment, print receipts and manage stock. It is a tool for shop employees, not a consumer app.

This policy explains what information Brave POS handles, why, and who else sees it. It covers the Android application and the Brave POS backend at pos.rollingpinn.com.

The data controller is Brave Brand Company Limited, 55 Bio House, 2nd Floor, Soi Sukhumvit 39 (Phrom Phong), Sukhumvit Road, Khlong Tan Nuea Sub-district, Vadhana District, Bangkok, Thailand. Business Registration Number 0105563083534.

Information we handle

Staff account information

When a shop creates an account for an employee, we store their name, email address, username, assigned role and branch, and the time they last signed in. Passwords and till PINs are stored only as one-way cryptographic hashes — they cannot be read back or recovered by anyone, including us.

Customer information entered by shop staff

Shops may record customer details in order to issue tax invoices and operate loyalty programmes. Depending on what the shop chooses to collect, this can include name, telephone number, email address, postal address, date of birth, gender, tax identification number, and a history of that customer's purchases.

This information is entered by shop staff, on behalf of the shop, with the customer present. Brave POS does not obtain it from any other source and does not buy, sell or rent it.

Transaction records

Every sale creates a record: the items sold, prices, discounts, VAT, service charge, payment method, amount paid, the member of staff who rang it up, and the date and time. These records are the shop's accounting data and are retained as described below.

Payment information

We never see or store card numbers. Card and QR payments are completed on a payment page hosted by our payment providers. Brave POS receives only the provider's transaction reference and the result, and stores those alongside the order so the payment can be reconciled or refunded.

Diagnostic information

To find and fix crashes, the app sends error reports to Sentry. These contain the device model, Android version, app version, and a technical stack trace of what the software was doing when it failed.

Photos and camera

The app asks for access to the camera or photo library only when a member of staff chooses to attach a picture to a product in the catalogue. The selected image is uploaded and used for nothing else. No other photos are read.

What we do not collect

  • No location or GPS data
  • No contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs or microphone access
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind
  • No tracking of users across other apps or websites
  • No card numbers, CVV codes or bank credentials

We do not sell personal data, and we never have.

Who else receives this information

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the system, and only the data each one needs to do its job:

Omise / Beam Payment processing. Receive the payment amount and order reference; they, not us, handle the card details.
PEAK Accounting Thai bookkeeping software. Receives sales documents where a shop has enabled accounting integration.
Loyalty service Where a shop enables loyalty, member identifiers and purchase totals are exchanged with our customer relationship system in order to award and redeem points.
Sentry Crash and performance diagnostics. Receives the technical error reports described above.
Expo (EAS) Delivers software updates to installed apps. Receives no customer or transaction data.

We also disclose information where Thai law requires it — for example, tax records provided to the Revenue Department.

Where data is stored, and for how long

Data is held on our servers in Europe and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords and PINs are hashed. Access to shop data requires a login and is limited by role.

Sales and tax records are retained for at least five years, as Thai tax law requires. Customer records are kept until the shop deletes them or the customer asks for removal. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

Your rights

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), you may ask us to give you a copy of your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict how it is used, or object to its use. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on it, and you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee.

Note that we may be unable to delete records we are legally required to keep, such as issued tax invoices.

Children

Brave POS is a workplace tool for shop staff. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or who we share it with, we will update this page and the date below.
