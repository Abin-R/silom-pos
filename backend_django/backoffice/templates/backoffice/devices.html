How this works
Each tablet reports its model, Android version, app version and update with the calls it already makes while someone is signed in, and this page keeps one row per tablet. A signed-in tablet is at most about a minute out of date.
Latest / Behind compare a tablet's update with the newest one any tablet on the same app version and system (Android or iPad) is running. Unknown means the tablet's app is too old to report yet — it fills in once the tablet installs the next update. Old app is the sideloaded build that never receives updates; reinstall it from Google Play.
The update number matches the one on the tablet's menu bar and in Settings → About (OTA …b32167).
